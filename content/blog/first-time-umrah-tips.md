To perform Umrah you complete four steps in order: enter ihram with the intention at the miqat, make tawaf by walking seven times around the Kaaba, perform Sa'i by walking seven times between Safa and Marwah, then shave or trim your hair. That is the whole rite, and most pilgrims finish it in a single visit to Masjid al-Haram. This guide explains how to perform Umrah step by step, with the practical details a first-time pilgrim from Australia needs.

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said, "(The performance of) Umrah is an expiation for the sins committed (between it and the previous one)" ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1773](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1773)). Learn the steps before you go, so that on the day your mind is on worship and not on what comes next.

## How to perform Umrah: the four steps

The Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah lists the rites on its [Nusuk Umrah journey page](https://umrah.nusuk.sa/Journey) as ihram, tawaf, Sa'i between Safa and Marwah, and shaving or trimming the hair. Here they are at a glance.

| Step | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ihram | Before the miqat | Wash, put on ihram clothing, make the intention for Umrah, recite the Talbiyah |
| 2. Tawaf | Around the Kaaba | Seven anticlockwise circuits starting at the Black Stone, then two rak'ahs |
| 3. Sa'i | Between Safa and Marwah | Seven walks, starting at Safa and ending at Marwah |
| 4. Halq or taqsir | A barber near the Haram or your hotel | Men shave or shorten the hair, women trim the ends |

The order comes from the Sunnah. Ibn Umar said that the Prophet (peace be upon him) arrived in Makkah, went around the Kaaba seven times, prayed two rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim, then went between Safa and Marwah ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1623](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1623)).

## Step 1: How do you enter ihram, and where is the miqat from Australia?

Ihram is the state you enter for Umrah, not only the clothing. You prepare by washing, as you would for ghusl. Men then wear two plain unstitched cloths, one around the waist and one over the shoulders. Women wear their ordinary modest clothing. You make the intention for Umrah and begin the Talbiyah:

*Labbayka Allahumma labbayk, labbayka la sharika laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.*

This is the Talbiyah of the Prophet (peace be upon him) as Ibn Umar reported it ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1549](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1549)). Men say it aloud and women quietly. Keep repeating it on the way to Makkah.

Once you are in ihram, certain things are off limits until you finish: perfume, cutting hair or nails, and marital relations, and for men, stitched clothing and covering the head. If you slip on one of these, tell the group's Shaykh, who will advise you on what to do.

### The miqat when you fly

The miqat is the boundary you must not cross without ihram if you are travelling for Umrah. The Prophet (peace be upon him) fixed these points for each direction of travel, including Dhul Hulayfah for the people of Madinah, and said they apply to anyone who passes through them intending Hajj or Umrah ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1524](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1524)).

Whatever your route from Australia, what you do depends on where you land:

- **Flying to Jeddah, then straight to Makkah.** The aircraft crosses the miqat before it lands. Men should be wearing ihram before that point, so change at home, or at the transit airport before the last flight. Make your intention when the crew or your group leader announces that the miqat is close. Do not leave it until Jeddah.
- **Flying to Madinah first.** You travel in normal clothes and spend your days in Madinah. On the day the group leaves for Makkah, you enter ihram at Dhul Hulayfah, the miqat on the way out of Madinah, where the group stops to prepare.

Pack your ihram cloths in your carry-on bag, never in checked luggage. A delayed suitcase should not be the reason you arrive unprepared.

## Step 2: How do you perform tawaf?

Tawaf is seven circuits around the Kaaba, walking anticlockwise with the Kaaba on your left. Be in wudu before you begin. Stop reciting the Talbiyah when you start.

1. Enter the mataf, the open area around the Kaaba, and go to the line of the Black Stone.
2. Men uncover the right shoulder by passing the upper cloth under the right arm, for this tawaf only.
3. Face the Black Stone, raise your hand towards it and say "Allahu Akbar". You do not need to push through the crowd to touch or kiss it.
4. Walk around the Kaaba. Men walk briskly in the first three circuits where there is room, and at a normal pace in the last four, as the Prophet (peace be upon him) did ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1604](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1604)). Women walk normally throughout.
5. Each time you come level with the Black Stone, raise your hand, say "Allahu Akbar" and begin the next circuit.
6. After the seventh circuit, cover both shoulders and pray two rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim if there is space, or anywhere in the mosque if there is not.

Make dua in your own language as you walk, recite Qur'an, or remember Allah quietly. A small card with the duas you want to make for your family is more useful than a booklet you are trying to read while walking.

If the ground level is packed, the upper floors are calmer, though each circuit is longer. If the call to a congregational prayer comes mid-tawaf, pray with everyone, then carry on from where you stopped. Losing count or losing wudu happens to many people: ask the Shaykh rather than a stranger.

Many pilgrims drink Zamzam water after the two rak'ahs.

## Step 3: How do you perform Sa'i between Safa and Marwah?

Sa'i remembers Hajar, the mother of Ismail (peace be upon them), who went between these two hills looking for water. Allah says: "Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah" ([Qur'an 2:158](https://quran.com/2/158)).

Signs inside the mosque point to Safa. The hills are now inside a long, air-conditioned gallery, with lanes for wheelchairs.

1. Begin at Safa. Face the Kaaba, raise your hands and make dua.
2. Walk towards Marwah. Between the two green markers, men jog and women keep walking.
3. At Marwah, face the Kaaba and make dua again. That is one of seven.
4. Walk back to Safa. That is two.
5. Continue until you finish the seventh at Marwah.

A common mistake is counting Safa to Marwah and back as one. It is two, so you finish at Marwah, not at Safa. Sa'i is a long walk on a hard floor. Rest between laps if you need to, and use a wheelchair if walking is difficult.

## Step 4: How do you finish Umrah, by shaving or trimming?

You leave ihram by cutting the hair. Men shave the whole head or shorten the hair all over. The Prophet (peace be upon him) made dua for those who shave before those who shorten ([Sahih al-Bukhari 1727](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1727)). Women gather their hair and trim about one to two centimetres from the ends.

There are barbers near the Haram, and Nusuk notes that you may also wait and cut your hair back at your accommodation. The restrictions of ihram stay in place until it is done. Once it is, your Umrah is complete.

## What do first-time pilgrims get wrong?

- **Crossing the miqat without ihram.** This is the mistake that is hardest to fix afterwards. Decide before you fly where you will change.
- **Arriving without a permit.** An Umrah permit is booked in the Nusuk or Tawakkalna app, you can hold one active permit at a time, and the [Ministry's FAQ](https://www.haj.gov.sa/en/Faqs) asks you to keep to the time shown on it. The same page gives five as the minimum age for a permit.
- **Rushing.** Nothing is gained by finishing quickly. Go at the pace of the slowest person with you.
- **Losing the group.** Agree on a meeting point with a gate number before you enter, and keep the hotel card on you.
- **Wearing new sandals.** Break them in at home. Blisters on day one affect the whole trip.
